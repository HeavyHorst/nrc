MODERN WITHOUT LOSING THE GRID

DESIGN SYSTEM ↗

All 24 color themes stay. These treatments change only border hierarchy, surface depth, spacing, typography, and emphasis while structure, density, square geometry, and explicit state remain fixed.

AMP ↗
Low-contrast panel rules, layered charcoal surfaces, compact metadata, and stronger emphasis only on active controls.
ORIGINAL REFERENCES
One clear outer frame, translucent surfaces, regular gutters, muted internal borders, and a single theme-derived selection accent.
NRC CONSTRAINT
Every existing color theme remains compatible. No pills, decorative rounding, lost density, or hidden operational state.
01 / RECOMMENDED

QUIET GRID

Keep the frame. Replace most internal outlines with surface transitions and very faint rules. Interactive controls remain visibly bordered.

  • Panel borders at medium contrast; row separators at roughly half that strength.
  • Headers use a distinct surface instead of a louder line.
  • Active state combines a shallow fill with one 2px accent edge.
02 / AMP-INFLUENCED

LAYERED OPERATIONS

Use Amp-like layered surfaces derived from the selected NRC theme. Preserve NRC labels and matrices, but let spacing and elevation carry more hierarchy than borders.

  • Surface separation is derived from every color theme rather than replacing its palette.
  • Sans-serif values and titles; mono remains for labels, IDs, and status.
  • Tool-like rows become quiet blocks with one boundary, not boxed cells.
03 / ORIGINAL-REFERENCE

ATMOSPHERIC FRAME

Retain the strongest cues from the source screenshots: a visible outer frame, translucent themed surfaces, and a focused selection glow.

  • The outer frame is the strongest line; internal structure is deliberately quiet.
  • Subtle background illumination adds depth without gradients on controls.
  • Accent is reserved for selection, focus, and the primary operation.
RECOMMENDED PRODUCTION DIRECTION

START WITH QUIET GRID, BORROW AMP'S TYPOGRAPHY SPLIT.

This is the smallest change with the largest effect: soften content separators, increase surface distinction by 2–4%, retain stronger borders only for controls, and use proportional text for readable values while keeping NRC's monospaced operational labels.
